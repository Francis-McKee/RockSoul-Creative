# RockSoul Creative

A responsive, two-page business website developed to establish a professional online presence for RockSoul Creative.

**Live Website:** [RockSoul Creative](https://francismckee.com/RockSoul-Creative/)

## Project Overview

RockSoul Creative previously relied on word-of-mouth referrals to connect with prospective clients and did not have an established website.

The client approached me to develop a website that would give the business a dedicated online presence and provide visitors with an opportunity to learn more about the brand and its services.

I developed a responsive, two-page website using HTML and CSS, incorporating client-provided photographs and logos into the design.

## The Business Problem

Without a website, RockSoul Creative had no dedicated online destination where prospective clients could independently explore the business.

Its reliance on word-of-mouth referrals meant that people discovering the business through other channels lacked a centralized website to reference.

The objective was to establish that online presence through a website that reflected the client's existing branding.

## My Solution

I developed a two-page responsive website that translated the client's existing brand assets into a cohesive digital experience.

My responsibilities included:

- **Brand interpretation:** Analyzed the client's supplied photographs and logos to identify and extract a complementary color palette.
- **Website design:** Developed page layouts and visual styling, using AI-assisted design and CSS development during the process.
- **Frontend development:** Implemented the website using HTML and CSS.
- **Responsive design:** Adapted the website's layouts and styling to accommodate different screen sizes and devices.
- **Deployment:** Published the completed website under my own domain, francismckee.com, at the client's preference to avoid the expense of purchasing a separate custom domain.

## Project Deliverables

- Two-page responsive business website
- Custom styling based on existing brand assets
- Client-provided photography and logo integration
- Mobile-friendly layouts
- Live website deployment

## Technology Stack

- CSS

## Results and Impact

The project delivered RockSoul Creative's first website, establishing a dedicated online destination for prospective clients.

The website provides a centralized location for visitors to learn about the business and its services.

No website traffic, lead-generation, or conversion measurements were available for this project, so no quantitative business outcomes are claimed.

## Project Considerations

The client opted not to purchase a separate custom domain. Consequently, the website was deployed under my own domain rather than a standalone business domain.

This arrangement allowed the client to establish an online presence without taking on the additional domain expense.
